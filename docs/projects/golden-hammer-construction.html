Golden Hammer Construction Ltd

Website and logo design for a construction company, from an open brief to a complete digital presence.

Categories
Construction
Deliverables
Website & Logo Design

Golden Hammer Construction Ltd gave me full creative freedom to create its website and logo. I took responsibility for both, bringing the company’s visual identity and digital presence together in one project. The website uses a dark-and-gold palette, prominent project photography, and clearly separated sections for the company, its services, and contact details. Designing the logo alongside the website gave me the opportunity to carry a consistent visual direction across both deliverables.
